# RTL Design and Verification

This repository contains the Verilog HDL programs, simulations, notes, and assignments completed during the RTL Design and Verification course.

## About RTL Design

RTL (Register Transfer Level) Design is a method of describing digital circuits in terms of data flow between registers and the logical operations performed on that data. It is widely used in FPGA and ASIC design.

Using Verilog HDL, hardware can be modeled, simulated, verified, and later synthesized into real digital circuits.
